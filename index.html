01 / ABOUT

Curiosity is where every project starts.

Welcome to my data science portfolio. I am a student interested in how programming, data and creative problem-solving can help us understand what happens around us. A dataset might look like a long list of numbers at first, but behind those values are real questions. I enjoy exploring those questions and learning how to turn information into a clear explanation. This website gives me a place to share what I am studying and to keep track of the skills I want to develop.

One area I have been practicing is exploratory data analysis with Python. For example, when working with transaction data, I can look at transaction amounts, spending categories and customer activity to find patterns that might otherwise be difficult to notice. I am also learning why preparation is such an important part of a project. Before analyzing anything, it helps to check missing values, remove duplicates and make sure the information is organized correctly. These steps are not always exciting, but they make the results much more trustworthy.

I am especially interested in data visualization because a good chart can make a complicated idea much easier to follow. I want to become better at selecting the right chart for a question, explaining what it shows and acknowledging what it cannot tell us. I am learning several tools along the way, from Python and Matplotlib to interactive graphics on the web. In the future, I hope to use these skills across topics such as technology, business and finance. On my Projects page, I share more about the work I have been practicing and include a small interactive D3 visualization.
